Lesson 1 · Connected letters

Arabic is read from right to left. The shape of many letters changes when they connect.

بَba
بِbi
بُbu
تَta
تِti
تُtu
نَna
نِni

Blend the sounds

بَ + بَ = بَبَ

ba + ba = ba-ba

بَ + يْ + تٌ = بَيْتٌ

bay-tun

بَيْتٌ — a house

كَ + تَ + بَ = كَتَبَ

ka-ta-ba

كَتَبَ — he wrote

Lesson 2 · Quran reading

Read right to left. Tap Hear Arabic, listen once, then repeat slowly. Transliteration is a helper, not a replacement for learning correct recitation from a qualified teacher.

1. Begin with the basmalah

بِسْمِ اللَّهِ

Bismi llāh

In the name of Allah.

2. A familiar phrase

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ

Al-ḥamdu lillāh

All praise is for Allah.

3. From Surah Al-Fātiḥah (1:2)

الْحَمْدُ لِلَّهِ رَبِّ الْعَالَمِينَ

Al-ḥamdu lillāhi rabbi l-ʿālamīn

All praise is for Allah, Lord of all worlds.

Lesson 4 · Listen & speak

Listen to the phrase, repeat it out loud, then try speech recognition if your browser supports it.

السَّلَامُ عَلَيْكُمْ

As-salāmu ʿalaykum

Peace be upon you.

Speech recognition availability depends on your browser and device. If it isn't supported, listen and repeat manually.

Practice tips

  • Listen once without speaking.
  • Repeat slowly three times.
  • Focus on clear sounds, not speed.
